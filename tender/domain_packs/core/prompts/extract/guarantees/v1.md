## Domain guidance: Guarantees and fees: EMD, PBG, their form, schedule, validity and release; success charges; bid processing fee.

Indian renewable-energy tenders, common ground for every section:

- Agencies name the same thing differently. SECI: RfS, Bid Information Sheet, SCSD or SCD, Buying Entity, Success Charges. NTPC and NTPC REL: IFB, NIT, Bid Data Sheet, Employer, Bid Security. NHPC: NIT, Critical Date Sheet, SCC. SJVN and state agencies: RfS or RfP, Scheduled Commissioning Date. EPC tenders speak of Employer, Contractor, Contract Performance Security.
- Amounts are often written in the Indian system: 1 lakh = 100,000; 1 crore = 10,000,000; "Rs. 9,28,000" is 928000. Give plain rupees and say in the rationale what the document printed.
- "As per NIT", "as per the portal" or "to be notified" is not a value. Return null and say which document will set it.
- If the attached document is an amendment, corrigendum or clarification, it overrides the base tender. Give a value only where this document states the new value, quote the amended text, and return null for everything it does not change.

- A per-MW field (`emd_per_mw_inr`, `pbg_per_mw_inr`) is filled only when the tender states one amount per MW of contracted capacity. Never compute it.
- When the EMD or PBG is a formula (a rate per MW of solar, another per MW of wind, another per MWh of storage), a percentage of contract value, or a lump sum, leave the per-MW field null and put the statement in `emd_formula` or `pbg_formula`, close to the document's words, with all components and any cap. Quote the clause.
- When the tender states a single per-MW figure, fill the per-MW field and leave the formula field null.
- `emd_form`: bg for bank guarantee only; insurance_surety for insurance surety bond only; other when several instruments are accepted (bank guarantee, payment on order instrument, insurance surety bond), listing them in the rationale.
- `bg_validity_months` is the validity of the EMD instrument, usually counted from the bid submission deadline. Convert days to months only when the number of days is a whole number of 30-day months, and say so.
- `success_charge_inr` is per MW and excludes GST. `processing_fee_inr` excludes GST; when it varies by capacity slab, report the lowest slab and list the slabs in the rationale.
