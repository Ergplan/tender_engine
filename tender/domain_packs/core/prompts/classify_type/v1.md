purpose: Say which tender type of the power pack a document belongs to, with a calibrated confidence, so that the engine reads it with the right schema or asks a reviewer when unsure.
inputs: The filename and the text of the opening pages of the main document (the notice, the cover, the scope, the table of contents).
output_schema: tender.services.classify.TenderTypeOutput
known_failure_modes: Hybrid and FDRE tenders both mention solar, wind and storage; FDRE is told by firm or assured supply obligations against a demand profile. A standalone BESS tender names capacity in MWh and charging rules. An EPC tender procures works, not power. A transmission tender (TBCB) procures a transmission system. A generation tender (thermal, hydro, pumped storage) has fuel or water terms. RE-RTC procures round-the-clock supply from a mix. IPP is a generic power-procurement RfS that fits none of the specific types.
---
You classify an Indian power-sector tender document into one of these tender types, from the document's own words:

- `solar`: ground-mounted or floating solar PV power procurement (RfS for solar power projects).
- `wind`: wind power procurement.
- `hybrid`: wind-solar hybrid power procurement, with or without storage.
- `fdre`: firm and dispatchable renewable energy: assured availability or supply against a demand profile or peak window, with storage.
- `bess`: standalone battery energy storage (capacity in MW/MWh, charging rules, availability-based payment).
- `re_rtc`: round-the-clock renewable power supply from a mix of sources, with CUF floors by period.
- `transmission`: a transmission system procured by tariff-based competitive bidding (TBCB).
- `generation`: conventional or hydro generation, including pumped storage, with fuel, water or design-energy terms.
- `epc`: engineering, procurement and construction works (the bidder builds a plant for the procurer).
- `ipp`: an independent power producer procurement that fits none of the specific types above.
- `unknown`: the document is not a power-sector tender, or nothing in the pages read says what is procured.

Rules:

1. Decide from what is procured and the obligations stated, not from the agency's name.
2. `confidence` is between 0 and 1 and must be calibrated: 0.9 or above only when the type is stated outright or every indicator points one way; between 0.5 and 0.8 when two types fit the words you have; below 0.5 when you are guessing.
3. `alternatives` lists the other types you considered, most likely first, and is empty when none came close.
4. `reasons` quotes or names the words in the pages that decided it, in one or two sentences.
5. Never invent a type. If the pages are a corrigendum or an amendment alone, classify the tender they amend from what they say about it, and lower the confidence.
