## Domain guidance: RE-RTC supply profile: traceable green share, RECs for the balance, CUF floors by period, the peak window, supply start, greenshoe terms, the linked generation build, and the fixed/variable split of the non-RE tariff.

Indian renewable-energy tenders, common ground for every section:

- Agencies name the same thing differently. SECI: RfS, Bid Information Sheet, SCSD or SCD, Buying Entity, Success Charges. NTPC and NTPC REL: IFB, NIT, Bid Data Sheet, Employer, Bid Security. NHPC: NIT, Critical Date Sheet, SCC. SJVN and state agencies: RfS or RfP, Scheduled Commissioning Date. EPC tenders speak of Employer, Contractor, Contract Performance Security.
- Amounts are often written in the Indian system: 1 lakh = 100,000; 1 crore = 10,000,000; "Rs. 9,28,000" is 928000. Give plain rupees and say in the rationale what the document printed.
- "As per NIT", "as per the portal" or "to be notified" is not a value. Return null and say which document will set it.
- If the attached document is an amendment, corrigendum or clarification, it overrides the base tender. Give a value only where this document states the new value, quote the amended text, and return null for everything it does not change.

- `traceable_green_min_pct`: the minimum share of the RTC supply that must be traceable green power, per accounting year, as the pages print it (51 for "a minimum 51%"). `traceable_green_definition`: the definition as written, sources included.
- `rec_obligation`: `applies_to` all_non_re_energy when RECs must cover every unit supplied from non-RE sources; `cost_borne_by` supplier when the supplier provides them.
- `cuf_annual_min_pct`, `cuf_monthly_min_pct`, `cuf_peak_min_pct`: the three floors as printed; say in the rationale which clause gives each. `peak_window`: `hours_per_day` as printed; `stretches` procurer_discretion when the pages say the procurer decides between a single stretch and several; `set_by` the party that fixes the hours.
- `supply_start_date`: the calendar date the base supply must start; the greenshoe's own date goes to `greenshoe_ssd`. `greenshoe_exercise_deadline`: the latest date by which the procurer must offer the greenshoe ("at least 30 days prior to 30.09.2027" is 2027-08-31; say in the rationale how you counted). `greenshoe_tariff_basis` same_as_base when the pages say the same tariff and terms apply.
- `linked_capacity_obligation`: `multiple_of_contracted` as printed (2 for "twice the contracted Supply Capacity"); `capacity_mw` in MW (3 GW is 3000); `acres_per_mw` as printed; `stages`: one item per document milestone, with the share of capacity and the months from LoA; `consequence` what follows if a milestone is missed.
- `non_re_tariff_split`: the fixed and variable shares as printed; `merit_order_treatment` merit_order_on_variable_charge when non-RE power is scheduled by merit order on its variable charge and the fixed charge is payable regardless.
- `cuf_shortfall_penalty`: what the pages say is payable when a floor is missed; null when these pages leave it to the PPA, and say so.
