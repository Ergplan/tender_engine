- 2026-10-04 Stage 1: candidates, approvals and canonical facts are attached to an object (`object_type`, `object_id`, `object_version`), not to a document. In Stage 1 the object is the document; Stage 2 passes the tender and its version number. This keeps core free of tender tables and lets Stage 2 attach canonical facts to a tender version; in Stage 1 the version is always 1 and no tender version exists.
