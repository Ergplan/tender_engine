- 2026-10-04 Stage 2: evidence location gains a fourth matcher (`core.services.evidence._locate_interleaved`), after exact, fuzzy and reordered: the quote's words found in order with other words between them, each within 14 page words of the one before. At least 90% of the quote's words must be found and every word that holds a digit must be among them. Reason: amendments are two-column tables ("existing clause | amended clause") that the text layer reads line by line across both columns, so a quote of the amended column never appears as a run of consecutive words; on the pilot tender 7 of 11 amendment values had no located evidence. The box is the union of the matched words, which is the quoted column. Trade-off: a quote that leaves words out of a passage is also located (its words are all there, in order), and its score is the share of quote words found, not a text similarity.
